Overview

The gates were too large to assemble inside the chamber, so they were fabricated and load tested on the quayside, then lifted in during one planned closure of the waterway. The drive machinery was commissioned dry before the chamber was flooded again.

The challenge

The original lock gates were at the end of their life, and the waterway could only be closed once, for a short planned period. The new gates were too large to assemble inside the chamber.

Our approach

We fabricated the gates on the quayside, load tested them there, and lifted them in during a single planned closure. The drive machinery was installed and commissioned dry before the chamber was flooded again, so the lock reopened working.

What we delivered

  • Gate structure and seal design
  • Quayside fabrication and load testing
  • Heavy lift planning for installation
  • Drive machinery and controls
  • Dry commissioning and reopening

The outcome

The lock reopened on the planned day, and the new gates are designed for a hundred years of service with seals that can be changed from the lock side.
