Overview

A distribution shed is a simple structure on an unforgiving programme. Connections were detailed to the fabricator's own shop standards, steel arrived on site in erection order, and the floor slab was poured in bays that followed the frame rather than waiting for it.

The challenge

A logistics developer with a tenant already signed needed a 58,000 square metre distribution centre handed over in fifteen months, from a greenfield site nobody had yet investigated.

Our approach

Ground investigation, foundation design and steel procurement ran in parallel rather than in sequence. Connections were detailed to the fabricator's own shop standards, steel arrived in erection order, and the floor slab was poured in bays that followed the frame rather than waiting for it.

What we delivered

  • Ground investigation and foundation design
  • Portal frame and roof structure
  • Steel procurement and erection planning
  • Floor slab design for high-bay racking
  • Coordination of the tenant fit-out

The outcome

The building was handed over in fifteen months, on the date the tenant had planned their move around, with the slab inside the flatness tolerance their racking demanded.
