Overview

Ridgeway needed to cut the energy draw of an ageing continuous line without stopping it. We metered the plant for a full season, calibrated a model against what we measured, and phased the heat recovery installation across four planned maintenance windows so the line never went down.

The challenge

Ridgeway's continuous process line was drawing far more energy than its design figures, and the plant could not be stopped to find out why. Any upgrade had to be installed around a production schedule that runs every day of the year.

Our approach

We metered the line for a full season and built a model calibrated against what we measured, which showed where heat was being thrown away. The heat recovery package was designed around the plant's four planned maintenance windows, with every tie-in prefabricated and rehearsed on the model before the line was opened.

What we delivered

  • Energy audit and a season of metering
  • Calibrated process and thermal model
  • Heat recovery and piping design
  • Shutdown planning and tie-in rehearsal
  • Performance testing against the baseline

The outcome

Energy use on the line fell by 35 percent against the measured baseline, the installation finished inside its four windows, and production never stopped. The model now sits with the operator's team for planning future changes.
