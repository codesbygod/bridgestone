Overview

The expansion needed 38 tie-ins to systems that could only be opened during the annual outage. Modules were built and tested off site, every cut was rehearsed in the model first, and the lift plan was sequenced so no two tie-ins ever waited on the same crane.

The challenge

Northfield's expansion had to connect to systems that can only be opened during the annual outage, and that outage lasts eleven days. Anything not finished inside the window would have to wait another year.

Our approach

We designed the expansion as fourteen modules, built and pressure tested off site while the plant kept running. Every one of the 38 tie-ins was rehearsed in the model and dry-run on site, and the lift plan was sequenced so that no two tie-ins ever waited for the same crane.

What we delivered

  • Expansion layout and module design
  • Off-site fabrication and pressure testing
  • Outage planning and tie-in rehearsal
  • Heavy lift and crane planning
  • Commissioning and operator training

The outcome

All 38 tie-ins were completed inside the eleven-day outage, the new plant was commissioned on the first attempt, and the works finished with no lost-time incidents.
