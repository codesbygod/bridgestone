Overview

Kingsgate sits hard against two listed facades and a live utility corridor. A secant pile wall propped at three levels holds the ground, and the neighbouring buildings were monitored around the clock, with trigger levels agreed with their owners before the first bucket of spoil came out.

The challenge

Kingsgate sits hard against two listed facades and a live utility corridor. Digging four levels down meant holding back the ground within millimetres, while the buildings next door stayed open for business.

Our approach

A secant pile wall propped at three levels holds the ground, designed with the movement limits agreed with the neighbours' engineers before work began. Automated survey prisms and tilt sensors reported around the clock, with trigger levels that paused excavation automatically if movement approached the limit.

What we delivered

  • Ground investigation and movement assessment
  • Secant pile wall and propping design
  • Monitoring scheme and trigger levels
  • Party wall agreements and neighbour liaison
  • Basement slab and waterproofing

The outcome

The maximum movement recorded at the listed facades was 6 millimetres, well inside the agreed limit, and excavation was never paused by a trigger. The basement was handed over dry and on programme.
