Overview

The crossing carries a dual carriageway and a light rail line over a tidal estuary with strong cross winds and an aggressive marine climate. The deck section was refined in the wind tunnel, the pylons were slipformed through two winters, and every stay anchorage was detailed so it can be inspected and replaced without closing the bridge.

The challenge

A tidal estuary with strong cross winds, a navigation channel that had to stay open to shipping, and a marine climate that attacks steel and concrete alike. The bridge also carries light rail, which puts tight limits on how far the deck may move.

Our approach

The deck section was refined in the wind tunnel until it stayed stable in the worst gusts on record. The pylons were slipformed in continuous pours through two winters, and the deck was built by balanced cantilever out from each pylon, so the channel never closed. Every stay anchorage can be inspected and replaced with the bridge in service.

What we delivered

  • Bridge form finding and wind tunnel studies
  • Marine piled foundations and pile caps
  • Pylon and deck design, including construction stages
  • Stay cable specification and anchorage detailing
  • Load testing and opening certification

The outcome

The crossing opened on schedule, carrying a dual carriageway and twin light rail tracks, and cut the journey round the estuary by 25 minutes. Shipping was never stopped during construction.
